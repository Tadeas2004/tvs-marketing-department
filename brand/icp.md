# ICP

## The One Liner
I help owners of established, revenue-healthy service businesses, usually 5 to 20 employees, go from paying staff to manually chase invoices, sort through leads and inboxes, and grind through repetitive admin busywork, to running a reliable AI system that just handles it, so they save that time and money for good without having to hire more people as they grow.

## The One Person
The real-world anchor is the owner of one of the best-known bakeries in Brno, Czech Republic — first name unconfirmed (surname possibly "Done," to be confirmed at the actual audit visit), a warm lead through a friend whose dad owns the business. Tech-open, wants modern tools implemented in the business, not sold to on hype.

His bakery is bigger than the real target (early guess was ~50 employees, possibly closer to 25, the owner isn't sure and isn't anchoring the ICP to that number). The repeatable pattern behind him: an owner of an established, cash-flow-healthy service business, roughly 5-20 employees, operationally bogged down. Doesn't have to be a bakery specifically, could be any local or B2B service business with the same shape of problem — the industry label was deliberately dropped, the size and problem shape were not.

## Current State and What It Costs
Hypothesis, pending the actual audit visit, not yet confirmed: 2-3 admin staff burning roughly 10-15 hours a week combined on manual work — chasing invoices, sorting leads and inboxes, other repetitive admin and sales busywork. The offer's framing: hours saved times the staff's hourly rate equals money saved. This needs real numbers once the visit happens — update this section then, don't leave the estimate standing as fact.

## Dream State
A system he actually trusts enough to check its output instead of redoing the work himself or through employees — not a demo that impresses once, something reliable enough to run "for years forward." He gets the 10-15 hours and the equivalent labor cost back, without adding admin/ops headcount as the business grows.

## What Makes Them Click
- A free audit, framed as something nobody local is currently offering him
- A live demo: watching an actual invoice or spreadsheet get processed in real time, not a slide deck or a pitch
- A hard number: hours saved and the dollar figure that comes with it

## What Turns Them Off
- An automation that breaks or gets something wrong once. That's the fastest way to lose him — it sends him straight back to trusting people over the system, and he won't give it a second chance easily
- Leading with "AI" as the pitch instead of the outcome (standing rule already in business.md)

## Who They Are Not
- Someone whose own business isn't proven or profitable yet, who wants to pay in commission or revenue-share instead of a flat fee. Real example: a friend who wanted a content system built and also wanted the owner as an unpaid sales/marketing partner for 50% of unproven revenue. Both sides declined.
- A "tech curious" hobbyist with no real revenue and no real interest in scaling — engaged and fun to talk to, never actually buys.
- The outright skeptic who thinks AI is "flashy, no real value, no real purpose." Self-selects out fast, easy to spot, not worth chasing.
- A solo creator or personal brand running on small revenue. A real, adjacent idea, and close to the owner's own past Threads business, but explicitly parked — not this ICP, maybe a future one.
- A business whose main bottleneck needs voice AI or telephony infrastructure (24/7 phone reception, instant speed-to-lead voice calls). Real, valuable segments, but not buildable yet on the current n8n/workflow skill set. Revisit once that capability exists.
