# Friction Points

Pairs with brand/icp.md. Where the ICP file says who the buyer is, this file says what stops that exact buyer from paying, in his own words, and what actually gets said back across the table. Built pre-revenue, from the owner's honest best read of this buyer, not from real transcripts yet, see the "Their Words" note in brand/icp.md.

## The Five

**1. "Has this actually worked for someone like me, or am I the guinea pig?"**
Mechanism: it already runs on the owner's own business. This client gets the build completely free, so there's zero financial risk on their side while it's proven on theirs. In exchange, the ask is a review once they're happy.

**2. "How can you guarantee this doesn't just break?"**
Mechanism: heavy pre-launch testing against real or synthetic edge-case data, an error trigger that gets fixed immediately if something breaks, and the owner personally checking the automation's runs daily.

**3. "If I hand you my customers and this breaks, do I lose money and look stupid in front of them? What if it says something dumb to a customer?"**
Mechanism: the system is scoped tight. Anything ambiguous or low-confidence doesn't get answered, it gets flagged and routed to the business owner instead of guessed at. It can't embarrass anyone because it hands off instead of improvising.

**4. "How long till this pays for itself?"**
UNSOLVED. No real client data yet to back a number. Honest future plan: once a case study exists, point at its real time/money saved and estimate a similar window for the next prospect. Not answerable honestly today.

**5. "Can I cancel?"**
Mechanism: cancel anytime, no retainer lock-in. If the client is unsatisfied, the automation gets shut down and no further fees are charged. UNSOLVED sub-question: no refund policy decided for a build that's finished and working to the owner's satisfaction but the client still isn't happy with it.

## Strongest As Content Ammunition

Point 3, the routing rule. "It doesn't get the chance to say something dumb, it hands off instead of improvising" is a concrete, specific mechanism, not a vague reassurance, and it doubles as proof of the engineering-over-flash contrarian take (see brand/contrarian-take.md): restraint built into the system on purpose, not a feature that looks impressive, one that's actually built to not embarrass anyone.
