## The Funnel Split

**The split: 5 give / 3 prove / 2 invite, out of every 10 public pieces.** Landed here, not the 7/2/1 or 8/2/0 default, because the ICP (see brand/icp.md) already feels the pain, an owner already spending their own time or paying someone to chase invoices, sort leads, grind through admin, they just haven't connected it to a solution yet, and have gotten used to the cost as normal. That means proof carries more weight than pure reach: showing a real system working is what moves someone from "yeah that's annoying" to "wait, that's fixable." Invitations can also afford to be a little more direct, since the motivation to fix the pain is already there once the fix is shown to exist.

- **5 give:** useful, shareable, or human, no ask. Reach and recognition.
- **3 prove:** a real system working, a receipt, a lesson earned, this is the heavier lift here because awareness of the *solution* is the actual gap, not awareness of the pain.
- **2 invite:** points at the next step only, never the wallet. Never two invite posts back to back.

Worth revisiting once there's a reason to re-segment or re-permission it, not a current-business owned channel yet.
