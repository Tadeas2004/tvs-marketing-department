## The Offer

A custom-built automation for the one specific bottleneck an audit finds in an established business, named and pitched around exactly the pain it removes (a "Lead Follow-Up System" for slow response losing sales, a late-payment chaser for invoice-chasing dread, and so on), built and tested within about a week, starting at $497.

## The Promise

I help owners of established, revenue-healthy service businesses, usually 5 to 20 employees, go from paying staff to manually chase invoices, sort through leads and inboxes, and grind through repetitive admin busywork, to running a reliable AI system that just handles it, so they save that time and money for good without having to hire more people as they grow.

## What They Get

- One narrow automation built around their exact workflow, named and pitched to match the pain it removes, not a generic "AI agent" or "workflow"
- Pre-launch testing against their own real (or realistic synthetic) data, covering edge cases before it ever touches a live customer
- Post-launch monitoring with an instant-alert mechanism: if it breaks, the owner is notified immediately and fixes it fast, not whenever the client happens to notice
- For anything customer-facing: the client's choice, built in, of a human-in-the-loop confirmation step before anything reaches a customer, or fully automated. Backend-only builds (invoice processing, internal data work) don't carry this decision at all, there's no customer-facing risk to guard against
- The audit's real findings, the task, the hours, the dollar figure, as a concrete deliverable, so the client has a real number even without a proven payback-timeline claim yet

## How It Works

1. Free audit call. Concrete findings delivered: this task, this many hours, this much money, before the client has committed to anything
2. Access handover: POS, inbox, forms tool, whatever the workflow touches. The build clock starts once access is actually in hand, not at the handshake, said explicitly so a slow response on his end doesn't quietly eat into what looks like delivery time
3. 3-5 days of build and testing against real data
4. Launch, with monitoring and the instant-alert guarantee active from day one

## Timeline

About 7 days, said out loud (5-10 business days internally, up to 2 weeks as the outer bound with progress checkpoints along the way so the client feels tracked, not left waiting). The first result is one narrow workflow disappearing, not the full 10-15 hrs/week admin stack fixed, that's the end state the audit's findings build toward over time, not what shows up in week one.

## What It Costs

$497 is the starting anchor for a first narrow-workflow build, not a fixed flat rate. Price moves up or down from there based on the value the audit finds, the hours and dollars uncovered, never based on build complexity or hours spent building it. A harder build that saves less doesn't cost more than an easy build that saves more, it's priced the other way around.

**The anchor-and-scale rule, made explicit:** $497 is the floor, for a smaller finding, a few hours a week or a few hundred dollars a month uncovered in the audit. Price scales up toward $1,000 as the audit's finding gets bigger, more hours, more dollars, a workflow that touches revenue directly rather than just admin time. The scale isn't a formula yet, it's a judgment call bounded by business.md's own stated first-project range ($100-$1,000), because there's no closed client yet to build a real value-based formula from.

This is a placeholder, not the finished pricing model, and it says so on purpose. Once 2-3 real clients close, replace this paragraph with an actual formula (something like a percentage of measured monthly savings) built from real before/after numbers, not a guess. Revisit this the moment that data exists.

## What Happens If It Does Not Work

Cancel anytime, no retainer lock-in. If the client is unsatisfied, the automation gets shut down and no further fees are charged. Genuinely unsolved, not papered over: no refund policy exists yet for a build that's finished and working to the owner's satisfaction but the client still isn't happy with it.

## Friction It Answers

- **FP1 (guinea pig):** answered by specificity (naming his exact workflow leaks) and the audit itself as proof, concrete findings before any commitment. Not by the free build, that gets you in the room, it isn't why he believes you once you're there
- **FP2 (guarantee it doesn't break):** answered by pre-launch testing against real data plus post-launch monitoring with an instant-alert-and-fix mechanism
- **FP3 (says something dumb / revenue risk):** answered conditionally. Backend-only builds don't carry this risk at all. Customer-facing builds get an explicit built-in choice: human-in-the-loop confirmation or fully automated
- **FP5 (can I cancel):** answered by the cancel-anytime, no-lock-in guarantee
