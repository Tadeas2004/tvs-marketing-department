# Contrarian Take

The find-your-contrarian-take skill finds this: talk to claude about what makes you different, in your own words, and it interviews the take out of you. The thing you believe about your industry that most people in it get wrong. It's the spine of your content and the reason anyone remembers you instead of the category wallpaper.

Talk it in with claude. If it doesn't make somebody disagree, it isn't contrarian yet.

## The Take
One or two sentences, in your words, sharp enough to disagree with.

## Why The Common Way Fails
The mechanism, not just the claim: what the advice everyone follows actually does to people.

## Your Proof
The experience that earned this belief. A take without a scar behind it is just an opinion.

## The Rebuttal
The strongest argument against the take, and what you say back. Hires get challenged in comments; this is what they stand on.

## Where It Shows Up
The surfaces this take should lead: content opens with it, sales conversations lean on it, the landing page headline breathes it.

## Raw Material From Day 1
The brain-dump-your-business skill saves your answer to "what does your industry get completely wrong" here, word for word. The find-your-contrarian-take skill builds the take above FROM this quote, so it stays even after the take is written: the ore stays with the metal.

> I think they provide the AI as the thing to sell when they should not really frame it as AI whatsoever to the business they are trying to work with because AI is the vehicle. It's not the result or the dream outcome that the business owner wants. So I think they are just in terms of presenting presenting their solution. They should always say it either saves money or it makes money. Saving money is direct insights to saving time. So, yeah, the AI solution should always be provided like that. They should offer the dream outcome of saving or making money in whatever way, but AI should not be the thing that we are selling we are selling a solution to their problems that either saves or makes the money. That's it. So we could mention AI in the offer, but it should not lead with AI, if you understand my point. We should lead with the result, with the dream outcome, with the pain point that we solve, and AI is just the vehicle that we get there.
