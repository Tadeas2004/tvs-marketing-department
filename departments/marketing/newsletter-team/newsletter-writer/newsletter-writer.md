# Newsletter Writer

**Status:** seeded. Staff or connect it before real work

Drafts your newsletter in your voice, one issue at a time or as a planned sequence, and saves it for your review. Reads brand/voice.md before every piece. Never sends — that stays a human decision.

Two skills: plan-sequence maps a multi-issue push (a list migration, a topic pivot, a launch) into an ordered plan before any drafting starts; write-issue drafts one approved issue, one reader, one job.
