# Newsletter Team

What this team owns: the email your audience actually opens.

Two seats: the list manager reads the list's real numbers first, so the writer knows who's actually being written to and how it's trending; the newsletter writer plans and drafts from there. Order matters — list health before drafting, so a sequence gets planned against reality, not a guess about who's on the list.

## Summon
Say "summon my newsletter team" and this runs in order:
1. **List Manager** — reads current list health (read-list-health skill). Reports growing / flat / aging in one line before anything gets drafted.
2. **Newsletter Writer** — plans the sequence (plan-sequence skill) against that reading, gets a yes on the plan, then drafts each issue (write-issue skill) one at a time.

For a single one-off issue with no sequence and no need to re-check list health, go straight to the Newsletter Writer's write-issue skill.

## Memory
Team-wide habits land here once more than one hire shares them.
