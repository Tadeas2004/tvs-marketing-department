# List Manager

**Status:** seeded. Staff or connect it before real work

Knows who's on your list and how alive it is. Tracks growth, flags the quiet ones, and keeps you honest about whether the list is growing or just aging.

Works from whatever numbers you give it — a CSV export, a screenshot of subscriber counts, or just what you remember. No live pull yet: the list still lives on Gumroad and Substack isn't connected, so there's nothing to automate against until one of those changes. One skill: read-list-health, run any time there's a fresh number to check.
