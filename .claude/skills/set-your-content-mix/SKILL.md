# Set Your Content Mix

Set the owner's starting funnel split and file it, so every content hire knows what mix to produce before a single post exists.

## One question that sets the split

When someone needs what you sell, do they already KNOW they need it, or do you have to convince them there is a problem at all? Already know: lean 5 give / 3 prove / 2 invite. Have to convince: lean 8 / 2 / 0, with invitations living in owned channels only. Unsure: the default below.

## The starting split (out of every 10 public pieces)

- About 7 ask for nothing: useful, shareable, or human. Their whole job is reach and recognition.
- About 2 teach with proof: show something real working, a receipt, a lesson earned.
- About 1 carries an invitation, and only ever to the NEXT step (join the list, get the guide, take the assessment), never to the wallet. The wallet ask happens later, in owned channels, to people who already moved.

Two rules ride the split: invitation posts never run back to back, and anything that could sit on a coupon never ships to strangers.

## The interview

1. Open your last ten posts and read me the last line of each. The last lines tell the truth about the mix; most owners discover they were either all-ask or all-silence.
2. Where would a ready buyer actually DO business with you? What is your owned channel? If the answer is nowhere yet, that is a finding, not a failure, it is something to build.
3. What is the one next step a warm stranger could take with you today? That is what the invitation piece points at.

## Rewrite brand/channels.md

File their answers under a heading called The Funnel Split: the split they landed on, their owned channel (or "being built" if question 2 turned up nothing), and the one next-step invitation from question 3. If the file still has an old heading with a day number in it, like The Funnel Split (Day 3), rename it to just The Funnel Split while you are in there. This skill only fills that heading; the platform-by-platform section in the same file gets filled by separate work.

## The ending

Read the split back in one line: seven give, two prove, one invites. Skipped answers go to business.md's Still to answer list, same as always. Save the work to GitHub by running the github agent's save-work skill at departments/it-systems/dev-team/github-agent/skills/save-work/.
